Product to field reveal

Products sit on a red-lit stage instead of white, as Taylor asked in August. Hover swaps in a colour photo of the product at work, with two key specs on every card. The filter groups products by the job they do.

Ready now. Six of ten products have a field photo; the other four lift on hover until photos arrive.

Drag-through views

On the detail page, the main photo cycles through the angles we already have as the visitor drags or swipes. It feels like turning the product over, using real photos.

Ready now for products with three or more photos: Bucking Frame, BHA Frame, RCD Press, Flush Mounted Spider.

True 360° rotate

The CRT Tether Ring and Flush Mounted Spider images are CAD renders, so ProStar's engineers likely have 3D models. Exported as GLB files, they can spin and zoom in the browser with no special photography.
